WORKOUT OVERVIEW

Build a Stronger Chest

This chest workout is designed to help improve upper-body strength while training different areas of the chest.

TRAINING PLAN

Chest Workout Plan

Follow the exercises in order for a structured workout session.

01

Bench Press

A compound chest exercise focused on building overall upper-body pressing strength.

4Sets 10Reps 90 secRest
02

Incline Bench Press

Focuses on the upper chest while also engaging the shoulders and triceps.

3Sets 12Reps 75 secRest
03

Dumbbell Fly

An isolation movement that helps train the chest through a controlled range of motion.

3Sets 12Reps 60 secRest
04

Push-ups

A bodyweight movement that trains the chest, shoulders and triceps.

3Sets 15Reps 60 secRest
05

Cable Crossover

A controlled finishing exercise designed to provide focused chest training.

3Sets 12Reps 60 secRest
TRAIN SMART

Chest Workout Tips

Focus on controlled movement and proper technique throughout the workout.

01

Warm up properly before starting your workout.

02

Use a controlled movement instead of rushing through repetitions.

03

Maintain proper form during every exercise.

04

Take adequate rest between sets.

Train with purpose. Move with control. Progress consistently.

Your progress is built one workout at a time.
