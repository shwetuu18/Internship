Your First Race Starts Here.

You don't need to be the fastest runner to prepare for a race. You need a plan that helps you build confidence, consistency and endurance step by step.

01
BEFORE YOU START

You don't have to run every day.

Race preparation is about gradually improving your ability to run, recover and stay consistent.

If you're a beginner, a combination of running and walking can be a comfortable way to build your endurance. Give your body time to adapt instead of trying to do too much too soon.

FitLife Tip

Your goal isn't to finish every session exhausted. Your goal is to keep showing up.

You don't need complicated meals. Focus on regular balanced meals and staying hydrated.

Before Running

Choose something familiar and easy to digest if you need food before your session.

Stay Hydrated

After Running

4 Beginner Mistakes.

01

Running Too Fast

Your training runs don't need to feel like race day every time.

02

Doing Too Much

Increase your training gradually and allow recovery between harder sessions.

03

Skipping Recovery

Rest is part of training, not time wasted away from training.

04

Trying New Things on Race Day

Keep race-day clothing, food and routine familiar whenever possible.
