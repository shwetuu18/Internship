5-Minute Reset

Short on time? Try a simple five-minute reset with breathing, gentle movement and a quiet pause.

Try It
01 • BREATHE

Slow Down Your Breathing

Take a comfortable position and focus on slow, relaxed breaths. Avoid forcing your breathing. The goal is simply to create a quiet pause and bring your attention back to the present moment.

You can use a few calm breaths as a short break during your day or as part of your cooldown after exercise.

Simple Practice

Inhale gently → pause comfortably → exhale slowly → repeat.

Back To Relaxation
02 • MOVE GENTLY

Stretch, Release & Relax

Gentle stretching can be included after training or during a break from sitting. Move slowly and stay within a comfortable range rather than forcing a stretch.

  • Neck and shoulder mobility
  • Gentle upper-body stretches
  • Easy hip and leg movements
  • Slow post-workout cooldown
Back To Relaxation
03 • REST

Give Your Body Time To Recover

Training is only one part of a healthy fitness routine. Creating a calm evening routine and giving yourself enough time to rest can support your overall recovery.

Create A Calm Evening

Reduce distractions, slow down your routine and create a comfortable environment for rest.

Back To Relaxation
04 • RECOVER

Finish Your Workout The Right Way

Give yourself a few minutes to transition from exercise back into your normal routine. Gentle movement, hydration and a calm cooldown can make the transition more comfortable.

  • Slow down gradually after exercise
  • Drink water according to your needs
  • Include gentle cooldown movements
  • Give yourself time to rest
Back To Relaxation
05 • MINDFULNESS

Create A Quiet Moment

You do not always need a long meditation session. Take a few minutes to step away from distractions, sit comfortably and focus on your surroundings or your breathing.

Mindful Break
