NUTRITION GUIDE

Protein Guide

Understand protein and make smarter food choices.

Why is protein important?

Protein provides amino acids that the body uses for tissue maintenance, muscle repair and many important body functions.

Good Protein Sources

Vegetarian Options

Lentils, beans, chickpeas, tofu, paneer, milk, yogurt and soy foods.

Non-Vegetarian Options

Eggs, chicken, fish and other protein-rich animal foods.

Smart Protein Habits

Include a protein source in your main meals.
Choose minimally processed protein foods regularly.
Combine protein with vegetables and quality carbohydrates.
Spread protein-containing foods across the day.

Protein needs vary based on age, body size, activity level and goals. Avoid treating one number as suitable for everyone.

FITLIFE WELLNESS

Hydration Guide

Simple ways to stay hydrated throughout your day.

Hydration supports performance

Fluids help your body regulate temperature, transport nutrients and support normal physical performance.

Start Your Day

Keep water available and drink according to thirst and your daily routine.

Before Exercise

Begin your workout hydrated rather than waiting until you feel very thirsty.

During Exercise

Drink fluids regularly, especially during longer or hotter training sessions.

After Exercise

Replace fluids after training and pay attention to your thirst and recovery.

Hydration needs vary depending on activity, climate, body size and individual circumstances. There is no single daily amount that suits everyone.
