Build a Stronger Back

Develop back strength, improve posture and build powerful pulling ability with our structured back workout program.

BACK TRAINING

Your Back Workout Plan

Follow these exercises in order for a balanced back-focused training session.

01

Lat Pulldown

Focus on controlled movement while pulling the bar toward your upper chest.

3 Sets 10–12 Reps 60 sec Rest
02

Pull-ups

Use controlled repetitions and focus on engaging your back throughout the movement.

3 Sets 6–10 Reps 90 sec Rest
03

Seated Cable Row

Pull the handle toward your torso while keeping your back stable and controlled.

3 Sets 10–12 Reps 60 sec Rest
04

Barbell Row

Maintain a stable position and pull the bar toward your torso with controlled form.

3 Sets 8–10 Reps 90 sec Rest
05

Deadlift

Keep your movements controlled and maintain proper posture throughout the exercise.

3 Sets 6–8 Reps 120 sec Rest
TRAINING TIPS

Train Smart.
Build Strong.

Good technique and controlled movements are more important than simply increasing the weight.

Warm up before starting your workout.
Keep your movements controlled.
Focus on proper exercise technique.
Rest between sets when needed.
