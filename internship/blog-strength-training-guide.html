WHY STRENGTH TRAINING?

You are not training
just to lift heavier.

Strength training teaches your body to produce and control force against resistance—bodyweight, bands, machines or free weights.

For a beginner, the useful goal is simple: learn good movement patterns, repeat them consistently, then make them gradually more challenging.

BEGINNER RULE If you lose control, the weight or difficulty is probably too high.

AVOID THE GUESSWORK

Three mistakes that slow beginners down.

01

Going too heavy

More weight is not automatically better. If the movement becomes uncontrolled, reduce the difficulty.

BETTER: Build control first.
02

Copying advanced workouts

A routine designed for experienced lifters may have more volume and complexity than a beginner needs.

BETTER: Master the basics.
03

Ignoring recovery

Training is only one part of progress. Rest, sleep and regular meals help you return ready for the next session.

BETTER: Plan recovery too.
